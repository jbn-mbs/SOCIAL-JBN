# careerGUIDE fact sheet

The ONLY source of product claims for reel scripts. If something is not written here, a script must not say it.
Keep this file accurate: when the product changes, update it here first. Lines marked (verify) should be
confirmed against the live app before relying on them.

## What careerGUIDE is
- A career guidance platform for school students (around Class 9-12) in India, helping them choose a stream and a career.
- Students take assessments; careerGUIDE turns their results into career matches and a career report.
- Parents and schools can follow progress through dashboards.

## Assessments
- Many assessments are built as games rather than long forms.
- Games include Career City, the New City RPG, the Ambavan 3D village game, and behavioural mini-games such as
  Office Crisis and Project Rescue. (verify which are live before naming one)
- There are also management-style scenarios: ten-turn decision sequences with a scarce resource and people to look after.
- Scoring is rule-based and deterministic (clear rules, not a black-box AI guess).
- Results are matched to occupations using the O*NET occupational database.

## Career simulations
- Students can try a career simulation: they step into a role, make decisions, and see what happens.
- How a student plays (for example confident, balanced or cautious) changes the outcome.

## Career report
- Shows a student's top career matches.
- Shows skill gaps: the skills to build for those careers.
- Shows the reasons behind the matches.
- Reports never promise a job or an outcome.
- An AI coach can answer questions using the student's latest results.

## For schools (dashboards)
- Institute admins see the whole institution: students, teachers, counsellors, groups, and how far students have got
  (enrolled, started an assessment, completed a module, has a career report).
- Teachers see the students in their groups, who is stalled or not started, and class score averages.
- Counsellors see career interests, common skill gaps, and a list of students to follow up with
  (not started, stalled for 14+ days, left the guided flow, or answers flagged for validity).
- Parents see their own child's progress, top career matches and skills to build.
- Dashboards are read-only: they never change a student's record.

## Things a script must never do
- Promise jobs, admissions, salaries or guaranteed results.
- Use numbers (users, schools, accuracy, success rates) that are not written in this file. There are none yet.
- Show or describe a real student. All footage uses sample (demo) data.
- Diagnose anything about a student's mental health or ability.
- Use fear about exams or failure as a hook.
